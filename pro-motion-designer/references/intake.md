# Intake (phase 1)

Ask in one message, grouped, so the user can answer at their pace. Offer defaults so they can just say "yes". Record answers in `brand/INTAKE.md`.

## The questions

**Product**
- Company name, product name, URL.
- In one sentence: what it does, for whom, and the outcome.
- What must the viewer feel or do after watching? (sign up, book a demo, understand one feature, trust the brand)

**Delivery**
- Channel(s) and priority. Suggest formats per channel: LinkedIn / web / YouTube 16:9 1920×1080; Instagram feed 1:1 or 4:5 (1080×1350); Reels / TikTok / Stories 9:16 1080×1920.
- Duration. Defaults: launch/product film 45–60 s; social cut 15–30 s. At 120 BPM, 48 s = 96 beats, 30 s = 60 beats.
- Deadline, and who approves.

**Brand**
- Do you have a brand brief or guidelines? (If yes, get the file/link. If no, you'll draft one from the website + a short interview; tell them it takes ~30 min of their time to confirm.)
- Logo files (SVG ideally), brand fonts and their licence, colours if known.

**Product content**
- Can you give access to the product, or screenshots/recordings? Who can sign in? (You never type passwords; they sign in in the browser themselves.)
- Which feature(s) or flow must appear? Anything that's planned but not live yet? (Planned features can't be shown as existing.)

**Taste**
- 1–3 reference videos they like and *what* they like in each (pace, transitions, typography, sound).
- Music taste; anything to avoid.

**Constraints**
- Legal/claims to avoid, competitors (named or not), people/topics/imagery to avoid, languages, voice-over or not.

## After the answers
- Summarise back in a short table, list open questions, and confirm before phase 2/3.
- If they're unsure about something with a sensible default (format, duration, BPM), pick the default, say so, and move on.
